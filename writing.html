Where it lives

Written for LinkedIn, kept here.

New issues go out on LinkedIn first, because that is where the readers are and where the conversation happens in the comments. Every issue is then kept here too, so the archive stays easy to find, easy to search, and in one place.

What's inside

Field notes from the work.

  1. 01 Agency life.

    What running a small agency actually looks like from the inside. Retainers, pricing, client dynamics, hiring — the stuff nobody puts in a case study.

  2. 02 Marketing craft.

    Paid, organic, SEO, content. What's working, what's overhyped, and the short honest version of why.

  3. 03 Business, practically.

    Building a services business from Bhopal, serving clients in four countries. The operating choices and the tradeoffs behind them.

  4. 04 Founder notes.

    Slightly more personal. What the work teaches, what Saurabh is reading, and the things he is figuring out a year into running his own.
