5 Marketing Trends I Hate (But They Just Work)

Published
5 February 2025
Reading time
2 min
Written by
Saurabh Tripathi

Hey there,

Ever feel your eyes roll at the latest marketing buzz—but then you notice that buzz is actually driving serious ROI? Trust me, I’ve been there. Here are five trends I can’t stand, yet I can’t deny their power to deliver results.

1. Ephemeral Content

Stories, reels, disappearing posts… it’s maddening how fast they come and go. Yet, these fleeting formats tap into FOMO (Fear of Missing Out) like nobody’s business. Users rush to consume content before it vanishes, driving off-the-charts engagement.

Why I hate it: You need a constant stream of fresh ideas—it’s like being stuck on a content treadmill.

But it works: Brands that leverage ephemeral content often see boosted brand recall and more real-time interactions.

2. Clickbait Headlines

We’ve all groaned at headlines like “You Won’t Believe What Happened Next.” But guess what? People keep clicking.

Why I hate it: It feels manipulative, and let’s face it, the content doesn’t always deliver on the promise.

But it works: The curiosity gap is real. These headlines tap into the human psyche, generating higher click-through rates—key for brand visibility in crowded social feeds.

3. Influencer Marketing Overload

Influencers are everywhere—reviewing, unboxing, and endorsing products left and right. It’s almost impossible to scroll without a recommendation popping up.

Why I hate it: The market is saturated with sponsored content, and authenticity can take a serious hit.

But it works: Influencers with genuine audience trust can skyrocket brand awareness. Plus, micro-influencers often deliver surprisingly high ROI due to niche-focused communities.

4. Chatbots and AI Automation

Chatbots can feel impersonal. It’s frustrating when all you want is to chat with a human, but end up with canned responses.

Why I hate it: Nothing beats real interaction, especially when you have a complex issue or a burning question.

But it works: Chatbots handle queries instantly and round the clock. They cut labor costs, scale customer service, and can even upsell when done right.

5. Retargeting Ads That Follow You Everywhere

Ever search for a pair of shoes, and suddenly every site you visit screams, “Buy these shoes!”? It’s like being stalked.

Why I hate it: It can feel intrusive—and sometimes downright creepy.

But it works: Retargeting taps into an existing interest. It’s a reminder to finish what you started, often nudging buyers over the line. The ROI from retargeted ads can be significantly higher than first-touch campaigns.

Wrapping It Up

Look, these trends can be irritating, but they’re not going anywhere. They each tap into basic human instincts: urgency, curiosity, trust, convenience, and familiarity. If you want to stay competitive, you’ve got to embrace what works—even if it occasionally makes you cringe.

Homework for you: Pick one trend from this list. Double down on it for a month. Track your numbers, then decide if it’s worth the love-hate relationship. You might be surprised at the results.

Until next time, keep building momentum and always remember: even the trends we dislike can yield serious gains if used strategically.
